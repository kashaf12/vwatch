# vwatch

When a task involves a local video file (what happens or is said and when, finding a moment, checking for
glitches, reading on-screen text), use the `vwatch` MCP tools (`video_info`, `video_transcript`,
`video_signals`, `video_overview`, `video_seek`, `video_crop`, `video_listen`) and follow the method in
`plugins/vwatch/skills/watch-video/SKILL.md`: plan first, take the free evidence (info, transcript,
signals), skim once with `video_overview`, zoom only on surprises, and cite every claim with its timestamp
and the tool that showed it.
